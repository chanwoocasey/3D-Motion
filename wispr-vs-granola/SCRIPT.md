# fn Key Custody

**Script v4, for review**

- **Format:** 16:9 (1920×1080) for X, about 22 seconds, 100% English.
- **Captions:** burned in, with the speaker's name and a yellow highlight on each word as it's spoken.
- **Style:** low-poly 3D in the style of the EO reference reel. That means faceted shapes, warm lamp light with hard shadows, film grain, googly eyes, and robots with glowing screen faces.
- **Style test:** `out/style-test.mp4`.

## World and characters

The setting is a dark living room at night. On the back wall there's an EO poster and a window onto a city skyline. In front of the couch is a coffee table with a cup, a ball and a phone.

- **ME:** a low-poly guy in a green hoodie and a beanie, with a laptop on his lap. His googly eyes follow whoever is talking.
- **GRANOLA,** floating on the left:
  - An acid-lime faceted sphere topped with granola clusters, with a spoon stuck in it like an antenna.
  - Its mouth is a row of pixel dashes, and it keeps a little notepad beside it.
  - Deadpan, with half-lidded eyes, and never in a hurry.
- **WISPR FLOW,** floating on the right:
  - A faceted sphere wearing headphones with a mic boom, and an `fn` keycap worn as a hat.
  - Its mouth is a live voice waveform.
  - Fast-talking and desperate.
  - Its purple is a placeholder until I have the real logo and color.

## Where each joke comes from

- **Both apps default to `fn`:** Wispr Flow is hold `fn` and talk. Granola launched `fn`-key dictation on Oct 29, 2026. Wispr's help docs say the two can interfere.
- **"Unlimited words":** Granola's dictation is free with unlimited words.
- **"A year free":** Wispr offers Granola users up to a year free to switch.
- **Meeting notes and the UNO reverse:** Wispr launched a Granola-styled notetaker first, then Granola added dictation, which TechCrunch called an "Uno reverse".
